## 21. How do I get a birth certificate in Barbados?
*government-services · answered · 6.3s*

To get a birth certificate in Barbados, you can follow these steps:

1. **Eligibility**: You can request a copy for anyone whose birth was registered in Barbados, including yourself. If you are requesting it on someone else's behalf, you must disclose your relationship and the reason for the request.

2. **Cost**: Each certified copy costs **$5 BBD**. For individuals aged 60 and over, the cost is **$1 BBD** per certificate.

3. **Application Process**: There are two ways to apply:
   - **Online**: You will need a debit or credit card and an EZPay+ account. Complete the application form online, ensuring you have all necessary information ready, as you cannot save your progress.
   - **In Person**: You can also apply in person at the Applications Desk at the Registration Department.

4. **Information Required**: You will need to provide:
   - Your name, address, and Barbados National Identification (ID) number.
   - The full name, date of birth, place of birth, and place of baptism of the person named on the certificate.
   - The full names of the parents of the person named on the certificate.

5. **Processing Time**: A certificate is usually ready for collection in **5 to 7 business days**. In cases of medical or travel emergencies, it can sometimes be issued on the same day with proof of the emergency.

6. **Contact Information**: For further assistance, you can contact the Registration Department:
   - Phone: (246) 535-9700
   - Email: registrarsupremecourt@barbados.gov.bb

Make sure to check the official government website for any updates or changes to the process.

## 52. What is the history of sugar in Barbados?
*history-culture · answered · 5.6s*

The history of sugar in Barbados dates back to the 1640s when sugar cane was introduced, leading to a significant transformation of the island's economy. Initially, Barbados had a frontier economy based on small farmsteads growing cotton and tobacco, worked by indentured European labor. However, the introduction of sugar cultivation and the establishment of large plantations, primarily worked by enslaved Africans, marked a pivotal shift.

By the mid-17th century, the exiled Jewish community played a crucial role in introducing windmill technology for sugar production, resulting in Barbados having the second highest density of windmills per square mile in the world, after the Netherlands. The sugar industry became the backbone of the Barbadian economy, dominating until the 1990s when tourism began to eclipse it.

The cultivation of sugar cane and the production of rum became deeply intertwined with the island's identity and heritage, leading to the establishment of events like the Barbados Sugar and Rum Season, which celebrates this rich history. The Sir Frank Hutson Sugar Museum serves as a permanent record of sugar production in the 18th and 19th centuries, showcasing the evolution of sugar cultivation and machinery used in the industry.

## 56. What is the story of Bussa's rebellion?
*history-culture · answered · 5.0s*

Bussa's Rebellion, which occurred in 1816, was the largest slave revolt in Barbadian history. It was led by Bussa, an African-born slave, and was fueled by the intolerable conditions faced by enslaved individuals under the Barbados Slave Code, which denied them basic human rights and subjected them to brutal treatment.

The rebellion was surprising to the planters, who believed their slaves were content due to allowances for social activities and basic care. However, a growing sentiment among the enslaved population indicated that they were ready to fight for their freedom, especially as the political climate in Britain seemed favorable for negotiations.

Bussa emerged as a prominent organizer of the revolt, alongside other leaders, including the notable woman Nanny Grigg. Unfortunately, the uprising was poorly timed and ultimately failed, as the rebels were outmatched by British forces. The rebellion was suppressed violently, resulting in the deaths of many rebels and a crackdown on the enslaved population. Despite its failure, Bussa's Rebellion became a significant event in the history of slavery in Barbados and contributed to the eventual abolition of slavery in the British Empire in 1833.

## 58. What is tuk band music?

Tuk band music, also known as rukatuk, is a traditional Barbadian musical genre that emerged during the colonial era. It combines elements of African-based rhythms with European military music, utilizing instruments such as a double-headed bass drum, triangle, flute, snare drum, and the pennywhistle, which has replaced the traditional fiddle. This genre is symbolic of Barbadian cultural history and reflects the fusion of different musical influences that occurred after colonization.

The tuk band is closely associated with the Barbados Landship and has historical significance as it evolved when traditional Afro-based drums were banned. Although its popularity has waned over the years, tuk band music remains an important part of Barbados's cultural heritage and is performed at various events and festivals.

## 78. What is Harrison's Cave?
*visitors · answered · 4.8s*

Harrison's Cave is a popular tourist attraction in Barbados, known for its stunning natural formations and subterranean environment. It features a network of limestone caves formed by water erosion, showcasing impressive stalactites and stalagmites. Visitors can explore the cave via a tram tour, which allows them to see various formations and natural features, including a large cavern known as "The Great Hall." The cave is located in the central uplands of Barbados and is recognized as one of the island's natural wonders. Tours typically run daily, with the first tour starting at 9:00 am and the last at 4:00 pm. 

For more adventurous visitors, there are also eco-adventure tours that involve hiking and exploring less developed areas of the cave. Harrison's Cave is a significant part of Barbados' geological and cultural heritage, having been explored and developed for tourism since the 1970s.

## 80. What should I know about driving in Barbados?
*visitors · answered · 6.3s*

Here are some key points to know about driving in Barbados:

- **Driving License**: You need a valid driving license from your home country to drive in Barbados. An international driving license is not required.
- **Temporary Driving Permit**: Visitors can obtain a temporary driving permit by applying at the Licensing Authority offices in The Pine, Oistins, or Holetown. The fee is $10.00 for less than two months or $100.00 for two months up to a maximum of one year.

- **Road Conditions**: Driving is generally safe, but be cautious of narrow roads and potholes.

- **Cellphone Use**: Only hands-free conversations via Bluetooth are legal while driving; using a cellphone to your ear is prohibited.
- **Public Transport**: Besides driving, you can use public service vehicles (PSVs), route taxis, and regular taxis for getting around the island.

## 91. What exactly are Bajan fish cakes, and how are they prepared?
*client · answered · 5.8s*

Bajan fish cakes are a traditional dish in Barbados, typically made from salted cod mixed with flour, spices, and seasonings. They are often enjoyed as a breakfast item or snack and can be served on their own or in a sandwich known as "bread and two" (fish cakes in salt bread). 

### Preparation:
1. **Prepare the Salted Cod:**  
   - Boil 1 lb of boneless, skinless salt cod in 6 cups of water for 4 minutes. Discard the water and boil again in fresh water for another 3 minutes. Flake the fish with a fork after boiling.

2. **Mix Ingredients:**  
   - In a bowl, combine the flaked fish with 1 cup of flour, 1 tsp of baking powder, 1 diced onion, 1 small finely diced hot pepper, and chopped herbs like parsley, thyme, and marjoram. Add 1 lightly beaten egg and enough water (about 1 cup) to form a thick batter.

3. **Fry the Fish Cakes:**  
   - Drop spoonfuls of the batter into hot oil over medium heat, being careful not to overcrowd the pan. Fry until golden brown, monitoring the heat to ensure they cook through without burning.

4. **Serve:**  
   - Drain on paper towels and serve hot, often with a dipping sauce like Marie Rose sauce or traditional Barbados pepper sauce. 

Fish cakes are a beloved part of Barbadian cuisine, celebrated for their flavor and versatility.

## 99. Can I legally operate a food vending business from a vehicle in Barbados, and which approvals are required?
*client · answered · 5.2s*

Yes, you can legally operate a food vending business from a vehicle in Barbados, but you will need to obtain the appropriate licenses. Specifically, you will require a food business licence if you handle food in any capacity, which includes selling food from a vehicle.

### Approvals Required:
- **Food Business Licence**: This is mandatory for any trade or business where food is handled. The application will be reviewed by Environmental Health, and an inspection of your food business will be conducted before the licence is issued.
- **Temporary Restaurant Licence**: If your food vending setup is intended to operate for a limited time (up to 30 days), you may also need to comply with regulations for temporary restaurants.

### Steps to Obtain the Licences:
1. **Apply for a Food Business Licence**: Submit your application to the Environmental Health Office.
2. **Inspection**: An officer will inspect your vehicle and food handling practices.
3. **Compliance**: Ensure compliance with the Health Services (Food Hygiene) Regulations, 1969.
4. **Display Licence**: Once approved, you must display your licence in the vehicle.

Make sure to check the specific requirements and guidelines from the Environmental Health Office for operating a food business from a vehicle.
