## 21. How do I get a birth certificate in Barbados?
*government-services · answered · 6.5s*

To get a copy of a birth certificate in Barbados, you can follow these steps:

1. **Eligibility**: You can request a copy for anyone whose birth was registered in Barbados. For Barbadian citizens, copies can be requested at any time.

2. **Application Process**: You can apply in two ways:
   - **Online**: You need a debit or credit card and an EZPay+ account. Complete the application form online.
   - **In Person**: Visit the Applications Desk at the Registration Department.

3. **Information Required**: You will need to provide:
   - Your name, address, and Barbados National Identification (ID) number.
   - The full name, date of birth, place of birth, and place of baptism of the person named on the certificate.
   - The full names of the parent(s) of the person named on the certificate.

4. **Cost**: Each certified copy costs $5 BBD. For individuals aged 60 and over, the cost is $1 BBD per certificate.

5. **Processing Time**: A certificate is usually ready for collection in 5 to 7 business days. In cases of medical or travel emergencies, it can sometimes be issued on the same day with proof of the emergency.

6. **Contact Information**: If you need further assistance, you can contact the Registration Department at:
   - Email: registrarsupremecourt@barbados.gov.bb
   - Phone: (246) 535-9700 or +1 (246) 535-9751 (if calling from overseas).

To apply for a Barbados driver's licence, follow these steps:

1. **Prepare Required Documents**:
   - Valid passport-sized photographs.
   - Proof of your age and residence.
   - Declaration of medical fitness.

2. **Complete the Application**:
   - Apply in the prescribed format to the Licensing Authority.

3. **Take the Regulations Test**:
   - After your documents are verified, you will need to pass a Regulations Test. A handbook of traffic rules, signs, and regulations will be provided with the application form.

4. **Obtain a Learner's Permit**:
   - If you pass the Regulations Test, you will be issued a Learner's Permit.

5. **Take the Driving Test**:
   - After holding the Learner's Permit, you will need to pass a driving test to obtain your full driver's licence.

**Fees**:
- Learner's Permit: $80.00
- Regulations Test: $30.00
- Driving Test: $100.00
- Driver's Licence: $50.00
- International Driver's Licence: $65.00

**Contact Information**:
- Email: blasupport@barbados.gov.bb
- Phone: 1-246-536-0264 / 1-246-536-0265

## 52. What is the history of sugar in Barbados?
*history-culture · answered · 6.8s*

The history of sugar in Barbados began in the 1640s when sugar cane was introduced from Dutch Brazil. This marked a significant transformation in the island's economy and society, as sugar production became the primary cash crop, replacing tobacco and cotton. The sugar industry required substantial investment and labor, leading to the establishment of large plantations and the importation of African slaves to meet labor demands.

By the late 17th century, Barbados had developed one of the world's largest sugar industries, significantly impacting the island's demographic and economic landscape. The population in 1655 was estimated at 43,000, with a notable portion of African descent due to the slave trade that supported the sugar plantations. The industry thrived, with Barbados becoming a major exporter of sugar to Europe, which solidified its economic importance in the Caribbean region.

## 56. What is the story of Bussa's rebellion?
*history-culture · answered · 5.9s*

Bussa's Rebellion, which took place in 1816, was the largest slave revolt in Barbadian history. It was led by Bussa, an enslaved African who became a prominent figure in the uprising. The rebellion was fueled by the harsh conditions endured by enslaved people and the growing abolitionist movement in England, which inspired hopes for freedom among the enslaved population in Barbados.

The planters were taken by surprise, believing their slaves were content due to allowances for weekly dances and some social and economic activities. However, the oppressive Barbados Slave Code, which denied slaves basic human rights and prescribed severe punishments, contributed to the unrest. Bussa and other leaders organized the revolt, aiming to negotiate for freedom in light of the changing political climate in Britain.

The rebellion was quickly suppressed by British forces, resulting in the deaths of hundreds of rebels. Despite its failure, the brutality of the suppression shocked many in England and strengthened the abolitionist movement, ultimately leading to the abolition of slavery in the British Empire in 1833.

## 58. What is tuk band music?
*history-culture · answered · 4.6s*

Tuk band music, also known as rukatuk, is a traditional Barbadian musical genre that emerged during the colonial era. It combines European military-style instruments with Afro-based rhythms, resulting from the historical context of colonialism when traditional Afro-based drums were banned. The typical instruments used in a tuk band include:
- Double-headed bass drum
- Pennywhistle (which has replaced the traditional fiddle)
- Triangle
- Flute
- Snare drum

Tuk bands often perform at cultural events and festivals, and they are accompanied by costumed characters that have African origins, representing various cultural elements. The music is symbolic of Barbadian cultural history and is an important part of the island's folk traditions.

## 78. What is Harrison's Cave?
*visitors · answered · 4.5s*

Harrison's Cave is a popular tourist attraction in Barbados, known for its stunning natural formations. It features a subterranean environment accessible via a tramway, allowing visitors to explore its impressive stalactites and stalagmites, as well as underground streams and waterfalls. The cave is located in the central uplands of Barbados, at an elevation of 700 feet (210 m) above sea level. The main areas of interest within the cave include "The Great Hall," which is over 50 feet (15 m) high, and "The Village," where some formations have formed columns over thousands of years. Harrison's Cave was first mentioned in 1795 and was rediscovered in the early 1970s, later developed for public tours.

## 80. What should I know about driving in Barbados?
*visitors · answered · 5.1s*

- Driving is on the left-hand side of the road.
- The speed limit is 60 km/h (37 mph) in built-up areas and generally 80 km/h (50 mph) on the ABC Highway.
- You must have a valid driving license to drive a motor vehicle in public places.
- A Learner's Permit is required to obtain a Permanent License, which can be applied for at age 18.
- An International Driving License can be obtained by citizens of Barbados for driving in foreign countries, valid for a maximum of one year.
- The island has 1,600 kilometers (990 miles) of public paved roads and one main airport, Sir Grantley Adams International Airport, located in Christ Church.
