## 21. How do I get a birth certificate in Barbados?
*government-services · answered · 7.6s*

To get a copy of a birth certificate in Barbados, you can follow these steps:

1. **Eligibility**: You can request a copy for anyone whose birth was registered in Barbados. If you are a Barbadian citizen, you can request a copy at any time.

2. **Application Process**: There are two ways to apply:
   - **Online**: You need a debit or credit card and an EZPay+ account. Complete the application form online.
   - **In-person**: You can fill out a paper form at the Applications Desk at the Registration Department.

3. **Information Required**: You will need to provide:
   - Your name, address, and Barbados National Identification (ID) number.
   - The full name, date of birth, place of birth, and place of baptism of the person named on the certificate.
   - The full names of the parents of the person named on the certificate.

4. **Cost**: Each certified copy costs $5 BBD. For individuals aged 60 and over, the cost is $1 BBD per certificate.

5. **Processing Time**: A certificate is usually ready for collection in 5 to 7 business days. In cases of medical or travel emergencies, it can sometimes be issued on the same day with proof of the emergency.

6. **Contact Information**: For more details or assistance, you can contact the Registration Department:
   - Phone: (246) 535-9700
   - Email: registrarsupremecourt@barbados.gov.bb

For more information, you can visit the official government page: [Get a copy of a birth certificate](https://alpha.gov.bb/family-birth-relationships/get-birth-certificate).

## 52. What is the history of sugar in Barbados?

## 56. What is the story of Bussa's rebellion?
*history-culture · answered · 5.7s*

Bussa's Rebellion, which occurred in 1816, was the largest slave revolt in Barbadian history. It was led by Bussa, an African-born slave, and was fueled by the intolerable conditions faced by enslaved people under the Barbados Slave Code, which denied them basic human rights and subjected them to severe punishment.

The rebellion was sparked by a growing sentiment among the enslaved population that their treatment was unacceptable, and many believed that the political climate in Britain might allow for negotiations for freedom. Bussa, along with other organizers, including the notable female leader Nanny Grigg, planned the uprising, which aimed to achieve emancipation.

However, the rebellion was poorly timed and ultimately failed. The enslaved rebels were outmatched by British forces, leading to a swift suppression of the revolt. Many rebels were killed, and the aftermath of the rebellion shocked even British society, contributing to the growing abolitionist movement that eventually led to the abolition of slavery in the British Empire in 1833.

## 78. What is Harrison's Cave?
*visitors · answered · 4.8s*

Harrison's Cave is a popular tourist attraction located in Barbados, known for its stunning subterranean environment. The cave features impressive stalactite and stalagmite formations, which have been formed over thousands of years through water erosion of limestone. Visitors can explore the cave via a tram tour that takes them through various chambers, including a large area known as "The Great Hall." The cave is also home to underground waterfalls and streams, enhancing its natural beauty.

The cave was first mentioned in historical documents in 1795 but was seriously explored and developed for tourism in the early 1970s. It is situated in the central uplands of Barbados, approximately 700 feet above sea level. Harrison's Cave is recognized as one of the island's natural wonders and is part of the Barbados Tourism Encyclopedia's "Seven Wonders of Barbados."

## 84. What should I do to prepare for a hurricane?

To prepare for a hurricane, follow these steps:

- **Secure Your Home**:
  - Install shutters or board up windows.
  - Ensure your roof and foundation are secure.
  - Trim tree branches that could fall.

- **Emergency Supplies**:
  - Store at least 3 gallons of drinking water per person for 3 days.
  - Have enough non-perishable food (canned goods, dry crackers) for at least 3 days.
  - Prepare a portable first-aid kit and a battery-powered radio.
  - Keep important documents (ID, insurance papers) in waterproof containers.
  - Have emergency cash in small bills available.

- **Communication**:
  - Charge all devices and power banks.
  - Write down important phone numbers and emergency contacts.

- **Plan for Evacuation**:
  - Know the location of the nearest emergency shelter and have a plan for your family.
  - If you live in low-lying areas, prepare to move household items to higher levels.

- **Stay Informed**:
  - Monitor local radio stations for updates and advisories.
  - Keep listening to the Department of Emergency Management (DEM) for alerts.

- **Before the Storm Hits**:
  - Fill your vehicle with gas.
  - Bring in outdoor furniture and loose items.
  - Turn off all electrical appliances and remove TV antennas from the roof.

## 91. What exactly are Bajan fish cakes, and how are they prepared?
*client · answered · 5.7s*

Bajan fish cakes are a traditional dish in Barbados, made primarily from salted codfish, flour, and various seasonings. They are often enjoyed as a breakfast item or as a snack, commonly served hot and sometimes accompanied by sauces like Marie Rose or pepper sauce.

### Preparation:
1. **Prepare the Salted Cod:**  
   - Boil the salted cod in 6 cups of water for about 4 minutes, then discard the water. Boil it again in fresh water for another 3 minutes.  
   - Flake the fish using a fork after boiling.

2. **Mix Ingredients:**  
   - In a bowl, combine the flaked fish with diced onion, flour, baking powder, a lightly beaten egg, finely diced hot pepper, and chopped herbs (parsley, thyme, marjoram).  
   - Add water gradually until a thick batter forms.

3. **Fry the Fish Cakes:**  
   - Drop spoonfuls of the batter into hot oil over medium heat, being careful not to overcrowd the pan.  
   - Fry until golden brown, monitoring the heat to ensure they cook through without burning.

4. **Serve:**  
   - Drain on paper towels and serve hot, ideally with a dipping sauce.

Fish cakes are often enjoyed on their own or sandwiched in salt bread, a local favorite known as "bread and two."
